# I Won't Give In—She's Feiyao

**A Quarter Before Noon**

Yao Guang rarely left her study at this hour.

The general's residence aboard the Xianzhou was unnaturally quiet that afternoon. Sunlight filtered through carved lattice windows, cut into flecks of gold leaf that scattered across the documents on her desk, several already touched by ink. Yao Guang set down her brush and rubbed between her brows. Just as she was about to call for fresh tea, the study door opened.

Evanescia stood in the doorway. A draft from the corridor lifted several strands of her short pink hair, and her rabbit ears swayed faintly. She wore casual clothes, the hem tucked loosely into a pleated skirt. She looked as though she had just returned from an idle stroll, yet there was no trace of weariness on her face.

"Is our honored general busy?" Evanescia stepped inside, light in both pace and tone, though her eyes fixed upon Yao Guang with a little too much brightness.

Yao Guang put down the brush and leaned back, studying her without haste. "Busy or not, you have already come in."

Evanescia's mouth curved. She walked around the desk to Yao Guang's side and did not stop until she stood beside the chair, nearly touching its arm. Yao Guang tilted her face to look up. The angle lengthened the line of her neck; the skin visible above her collar shone faintly ivory in the afternoon light.

Evanescia looked down at her, something glinting in her eyes.

"What are you looking at?" Yao Guang asked, her voice steady with a general's habitual composure.

"At Boss Yao, of course." Evanescia reached out and straightened a collar that had not held a single wrinkle to begin with. Her fingertip brushed, as though by accident, across the skin above Yao Guang's collarbone—as light as a feather touching water.

Yao Guang's lashes trembled. The fingers resting on the chair arm tightened slightly.

Evanescia withdrew her hand, clasped it behind her back, and tilted her head. "Boss Yao has worked all morning. Aren't you tired?"

"Not particularly."

"Not particularly?" Evanescia repeated, the final syllable rising. Suddenly she bent forward and placed both hands on the chair arms, trapping Yao Guang between them. Their faces were so close Yao Guang could see the ring of violet pattern within Evanescia's irises, so close that Evanescia's breath fell upon her lips.

"Boss Yao~" Evanescia lowered her voice, sweetness and curiosity mingling in her tone. "Your ears are red."

Yao Guang gave a small, involuntary cough.

"Nonsense." Her voice remained calm, but the flush at the tips of her ears no longer obeyed her. It spread from lobe to rim, thin and translucent as though stained by the evening glow. Evanescia's gaze traveled slowly over those ears and finally settled upon her lips.

She laughed softly.

Then she released the arms, straightened, and walked to the tea table to fetch the pot, moving as naturally as if nothing had happened.

Yao Guang remained in the chair and tapped one finger against the armrest. She took up her brush and returned to the documents. Her script remained neat and precise; only the whiteness of her knuckles betrayed her grip.

Evanescia returned with the pot and refilled the cup at Yao Guang's hand. Afterward, she did not step away, but leaned against the desk and watched her write with open interest. A moment later, she reached out. Her index finger touched the back of Yao Guang's brush hand and slowly traced the bones toward her wrist.

The brush stopped.

"...Miss Evanescia..."

"Hm?" Evanescia's finger rested against the side of her wrist, directly above the pulse.

"What exactly are you doing?" Yao Guang put down the brush and turned to see what Evanescia intended.

Something inside Evanescia stirred beneath that gaze. Yao Guang's ears were thoroughly red; her pulse leaped faster under Evanescia's fingertip; the hand that had held the brush was clenched white.

Evanescia could not resist teasing her and seeing how Boss Yao might respond.

So she did something even she considered rather daring.

Beside Yao Guang's chair, she bent down onto one knee and placed a hand upon Yao Guang's knee. Yao Guang looked down, her brow faintly creased and lips parting to tell her to stand—when Evanescia's hand slid from her knee to the outside of her thigh.

Through the fabric of the qipao, Evanescia's fingertips sank into the soft curve. They followed the shape of her leg slowly upward and stopped near the top of her thigh, her thumb drawing the smallest circle upon the cloth.

"Evanescia—" Yao Guang's voice rose half a note. She reached down and caught Evanescia's wrist. Evanescia did not stop. She turned her hand and caught Yao Guang's in return, while the other continued to roam the outside of her thigh, palm gliding unhurriedly over the gentle rise beneath the fabric, back and forth.

"Boss Yao~" Evanescia looked up with the same innocent voice. "Your legs are so soft."

Yao Guang's ears were red enough to bleed. She pulled at Evanescia's hand, but Evanescia's fingers suddenly tightened and pinched the inside of her thigh.

The flesh was soft and sensitive. Even through the fabric, the twist made Yao Guang shudder from head to toe. She bit down upon her lower lip to smother the sound that almost escaped, but a thin sheen of water gathered in her eyes.

Evanescia looked up at her and laughed twice with perfect sweetness.

Yao Guang stared down at Evanescia crouched before her, unable to quiet the warm tide inside her chest. Her lips parted, but no words came.

"Big Sister Yao Guang." Evanescia rose, bracing one hand on the chair and sliding the other around Yao Guang's waist, then bent to kiss her.

Yao Guang's lips were softer than she had imagined, carrying a tender fruit fragrance and the faintest sweetness. Evanescia caught her lower lip and touched its warmth with the tip of her tongue. Yao Guang gave a muffled sound, fingers knotting desperately in the cloth at Evanescia's waist. Yet she did not turn away. Instead, her lips opened by the slightest measure.

Evanescia's tongue slipped inside. Yao Guang's mouth was warm and soft; when their tongues touched, her shoulders tensed. Evanescia did not hurry. Her tongue traced the line of Yao Guang's teeth, caught the tip of hers, and slowly circled it. Yao Guang's breathing turned quick and disordered, her knuckles white where she clutched Evanescia's clothes. Still, she did not push her away. When Evanescia entwined with her again, she relaxed her teeth and let the kiss deepen. Tiny wet sounds escaped between their lips, setting the heat at Yao Guang's ears burning all the way down her neck.

When Evanescia drew back, Yao Guang's eyes remained closed. Her damp lashes glittered and trembled like butterfly wings caught in rain. Her kissed lips were slightly swollen, fuller than usual and shining with moisture.

"Yao..." Evanescia tried to say her name, her voice rough. She pressed her lips to the corner of Yao Guang's eye and kissed the reddened skin. It was startlingly hot, faintly damp and salty.

Yao Guang opened her eyes. The gaze that was usually still as lake water now swam with mist.

She watched Evanescia, fingers still tangled in her clothes.

"Mm." Evanescia answered, letting her lips travel from the corner of Yao Guang's eye to her temple, and then to the shell of her ear. She took the earlobe into her mouth and ran her tongue around its thin softness. Yao Guang's shoulders drew sharply inward. A tiny breath hissed between her teeth; her waist instinctively arched before she shrank back against the chair.

Evanescia followed, leaning over her. One hand held the chair back while the other slipped from Yao Guang's waist to her lower stomach, the heel of her palm pressing slowly upon the smooth, soft plane. Yao Guang's abdomen tightened at her touch. Even through the clothing, Evanescia felt it drawn taut, like warm jade under a layer of thin ice. Her palm rested there, feeling the restrained tremor, then gently kneaded the tension away.

"Big Sister Yao Guang," Evanescia murmured against her ear, warm breath spilling into it. Yao Guang bit her lip, the red around her eyes deepening. Yet beneath Evanescia's palm, her stomach truly relaxed little by little.

Evanescia's hand continued downward, settling again upon the outside of her thigh. This time no outer fabric intervened. She lifted the hem of Yao Guang's robe and reached through its slit, fingertips touching the thigh beneath only a thin underlayer. The skin under her touch was smooth and cool, the muscle drawn faintly tight with tension, warm and soft and trembling almost imperceptibly.

Evanescia pressed her thumb into the yielding flesh and massaged it in circles, neither hard nor soft. Yao Guang's breath sounded beside her ear, quick and slow by turns. Every circle drew out a tiny, restrained gasp. In the still afternoon, the rustle of cloth and suppressed breathing rang unusually clear. Then Evanescia's fingers slipped to the inner thigh, and one nail traced lightly across its most delicate skin.

Yao Guang could not stop the cry that escaped. Her startled breath trembled at its end. She pressed her forehead against Evanescia's shoulder and twisted the cloth at her waist until her fingers shook.

Evanescia kissed the crown of her hair. Her fingers found the softest flesh again; thumb and forefinger closed and pinched. The pads sank into smooth softness, feeling fine skin slip and tighten between them. Yao Guang jolted upright, ground her forehead against Evanescia's shoulder, and released a low, muffled moan through her nose.

"Did I hurt Boss Yao?"

Yao Guang shook her head, loose hair whispering against the hollow of Evanescia's shoulder. Only after a moment did she answer, muffled and low: "...It doesn't hurt."

Evanescia could not hold back her laugh. She pinched the same place again, more gently this time, then immediately covered it with her palm and rubbed away the sting. Yao Guang's breathing broke again; her shoulders trembled, the lingering sensation spreading from the top of her thigh. A moment later, Evanescia felt a spot of cool dampness against her shoulder.

She looked down and saw a fine glimmer of moisture clinging to Yao Guang's lashes.

"Yao Guang?" Evanescia cupped her face. Yao Guang turned away and refused to look, but held between Evanescia's hands she could not hide. Her eyes were red, as was the tip of her nose; her lower lip was slightly swollen where she had bitten it, and the mist in her eyes was close to gathering into rain.

Something soft struck Evanescia's heart.

"I won't tease you anymore," she said quietly, wiping that trace of moisture from Yao Guang's eye with her thumb. "If you do not want this, I will stop."

Yao Guang was silent for a moment. Then she reached up and caught Evanescia's wrist.

"...I didn't say I didn't want it." Her voice was nearly inaudible. The tips of her ears had turned almost transparent with heat.

Evanescia blinked. Then she bent down until their foreheads rested together and the tips of their noses touched.

"Then you do want it," she said.

Yao Guang did not answer, but her lips brushed one corner of Evanescia's, light as a fallen blossom.

Evanescia stopped holding back. One arm locked around Yao Guang's waist, the other cradled the back of her neck, and she kissed her fiercely.

Her lips covered Yao Guang's again. Her tongue found Yao Guang's and pressed deeper, circling it several times before drawing it outward and catching it lightly between her teeth. Broken murmurs sounded in Yao Guang's throat. Her fingers slid into Evanescia's short hair and held the back of her head, pulling herself harder into the kiss. Evanescia's hand slipped from her neck and pressed downward, vertebra by vertebra, feeling through the robe the graceful hollow that ran from nape to waist. Her thumb pressed into the small of Yao Guang's back. Yao Guang softened and folded another half-inch into her arms.

Evanescia's mouth moved from the corner of Yao Guang's lips to her jaw and then her neck. She took a small patch of skin into her mouth and sucked until a crimson mark appeared, like a peach blossom fallen upon snow. Yao Guang tipped her neck back, chin resting on Evanescia's crown, breath swift and urgent. Evanescia traveled lower, biting once at the collarbone. Her teeth pressed into thin skin with a force poised exactly between pain and ticklish pleasure. Yao Guang shuddered, fingers tightening in Evanescia's hair, and gave a soft, nasal hum.

Evanescia kissed the tooth mark and lifted her head to find Yao Guang's mouth once more.

This kiss was long and slow, like waves after the tide had turned, rising and retreating in tender intervals that made the heart tremble. Yao Guang's tongue met hers shyly and wound around it with awkward care, every touch so earnest it brought heat to Evanescia's chest.

Neither knew how long had passed when Evanescia finally released her.

Yao Guang leaned against the chair, eyes half-open and unfocused, lips red and swollen. Color in every shade marked her neck from collarbone to ear. Her collar had opened, half revealing the upper curve beneath it, and the tooth mark upon her collarbone stood plain. Her chest rose and fell, breath not yet steady. She looked as though she had awakened from a very long dream.

Evanescia watched her, one hand braced on the desk and the other still around her waist, fingertips unconsciously stroking the small of her back.

"...Are you all right?" Evanescia asked, her voice rough enough to sound like someone else's.

Yao Guang raised her eyes. The mist had not yet cleared, but something else surfaced beneath it—tender, indulgent, helpless, touched with a sweetness that could not be named.

"What do you think?" Yao Guang replied. Her voice was hoarse too, though three or four parts of her usual manner had returned.

Evanescia smiled and kissed the tip of her nose. "I think Boss Yao rather enjoyed herself."

Yao Guang blushed again. She turned away and straightened her clothes with perfect formality, attempting to hide every sign of disarray. But the tooth mark on her collarbone and the crimson bloom on her neck remained. There was no hiding them.

The sight of her struggling for composure softened Evanescia's heart entirely. She reached out to arrange Yao Guang's collar, letting one finger press gently upon the tooth mark as it passed. Yao Guang drew in a breath and glared at her.

The look made Evanescia's heart miss a beat. Those eyes still shone with water, those lips remained faintly swollen—there was nothing threatening in it, only another invitation to bend and kiss her.

"Come to my residence for dinner tonight," Yao Guang said suddenly.

Evanescia blinked. "Is the general asking me on a date?"

"I am informing you." Yao Guang flexed her fingers. Seven or eight parts of her usual composure had returned, though her wrist still trembled slightly.

Evanescia studied her delicate profile for a moment, then bent to her ear.

"And after dinner?"

Yao Guang did not look up. Heat spread across her ears again.

"...We will discuss that afterward."

Evanescia laughed. She pressed a kiss to Yao Guang's hair, straightened, and walked toward the door. At the threshold, she stopped and looked back.

Yao Guang sat at her desk, posture restored to its former precision, robes arranged as though nothing had happened. Then she rested a hand upon the chair arm and touched her lower lip with one fingertip, as if the sensation of their entwined tongues still lingered there.

Evanescia smiled, slipped out, and gently drew the door closed.

The study grew quiet again. Sunlight had retreated from the desk to the corner of the wall. The tea had gone cold. Yao Guang leaned back and closed her eyes. The faintest tingling remained where her inner thigh had been pinched; her collar rubbed the tooth mark at her collarbone, and with every breath she felt the tiny mixture of ache and itch sink sweetly between her bones.

She raised a hand and pressed her fingertips to her lips.
