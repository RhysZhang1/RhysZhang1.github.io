# Big Sister Venus's Chocolate Cake

"Chocolate cake... I tried... making one..." Big Sister Venus's voice trembled. The tips of her ears were so red they looked ready to bleed.

"It's my first time... so it might..."

That flush swiftly spread from the tips of her ears across them, then down to her pink cheeks and fair neck. The rather clumsy cake, together with her evasive yet shining eyes, urged my heart to beat faster and faster. It was like a silent bomb bursting inside my chest.

I lifted my spoon, gently scooped out a small piece, and brought it to my mouth beneath Big Sister Venus's radiant gaze.

To be honest, it tasted awful. An overbearing, far too intense wave of chocolate seized every taste bud at once. Sweetness followed—sweet enough to scorch my tongue. Finally, the burnt crust joined the assault. Three flavors, none of them remotely pleasant, nearly knocked my sense of taste out altogether.

I swallowed with difficulty, rigidly controlling my face so that not a trace of discomfort could show.

"It's... it's really good..." With what felt like my final breath, I forced the words from my throat.

"Liar..."

Light as a feather, the word pierced every layer of my disguise.

I could not lie in front of Big Sister Venus. Her gentle gaze would melt away every falsehood in me.

I sighed softly.

She stood beside me, aggrieved and forlorn like a child who had done something wrong.

I smiled helplessly.

"Big Sister Venus, do you know what matters most in cooking?" I asked without warning.

"Isn't it... the taste?" Her voice dwindled until she seemed ready to disappear into a crack in the floor.

"It's the feeling behind it. Cooking is the act of passing your feelings to another person," I said gently.

"Huh?" She could hardly believe me.

"The flavor may be a little strange, but I can feel it. When you heated the chocolate and butter, you must have been imagining me eating it all in one bite. While whisking the batter, you must have pictured my eyes lighting up after I finished. The moment it was done, you must have hurried all the way here, hoping I could taste it at once. I can even imagine you pulling it from the oven too soon and burning your finger..."

My gaze settled on the right hand she had been carefully hiding. Sure enough, a bandage covered one finger where the baking tray had burned her.

"I can feel every bit of your heart inside this cake..." I reached out and patted her head through her baseball cap. "Next time, let me teach you."

"Can I... really...?" Her blush, mingled with the scent of chocolate, rushed into my nose. I could no longer hold down the corners of my mouth.

"Of course. Cooking for you will be my way of sharing my feelings—you silly big sister."

"I'm not... a silly big sister..."

Somehow, the two strawberries on the chocolate cake seemed to nestle a little closer together...
