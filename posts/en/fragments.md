The moon will bless you, by night and by day. 😌
I know this goes against your principles. 😥
I will not forget what I owe you. 🤗
I only wanted to express my gratitude. 😚
Thank you... 😘 You are an indispensable member of our company. 🥺

You would not complete a mission at any cost, would you? 😥 A little less mischief and a little more regard for safety will suffice. We will all do our best to cooperate. 😘

Nefer, these things... they are already in the past. 😞
Thank you for helping me. To me, this is almost... impossible to believe. 😁
I have a feeling Miss Nefer will help us until the very end. 😌
I trust her. 😊

Miss Nefer... are you not supposed to be calculating and shrewd? I cannot believe even you would act so impulsively. 🤬
Even so... 🥲 Ah.

From a merchant's perspective, would that count as a tremendous profit? 😡

To surrender what should never have been demanded is torment—whether faith or strength. 😰

## Sandrone and Columbina

**Sandrone:**

Co-lum-bi-na, what are you doing?! 😡 Who asked you to interfere? 😖 Do not drag me to some inexplicable place on a whim ever again! 😫

Hmph... It is not the quality of the tea that concerns me. 😞 It is that the guests at my tea parties have left one after another. 😔
First Rosalyne, and now you. 😢 There is no one left to amuse me. 😭

This is the first time I have met a centuries-old god who has drunk nothing but primitive tribute herb tea. 😒
Yes, exactly. What other reason could there be? 😌 It is one of the few refined pleasures in my tedious life. ☺️

What? When did I say I was inviting you? 😒
If you missed my tea so much, you should never have left the Fatui. 🥺
Under Her Majesty the Tsaritsa's protection, no one would dare oppose you as openly as they do now. 😭

Hmph... Your departure was entirely predictable. 😠 I understand why you left, and I have no interest in following orders from above to drag you back. 😘
But if you continue associating with these people, the next time we meet we may be enemies. 🙄

Staring will not earn you another invitation to my tea party, Columbina. 😤
Tch. This is exactly why I said you should not spend time with these people. 😥 You have learned nothing but sentimental, presumptuous nonsense. 🤗

You came all this way only to say that? 🙃
How novel. I am so moved I can hardly drink my tea... If you are finished, leave. 😒
Pronia, show Lady Columbina out. 👿

**Columbina:**

The margin of error in your "calculations" has grown rather large. Have you been taking proper care of yourself? ☺️
Try going to bed early and rising early, or perhaps what I recommended before... 😘

Mm... I am concerned for you, Sandrone. 😔
Like that time your research made no progress and you shut yourself in your room alone... 😌
I imagine you served them the worst tea you had, just as you did when I upset you. 😉

You prepare a different tea for me every time. 😋 Is it only to watch my reaction? 😥
I know... but... 😞 Thank you, Sandrone. 🤗

I used to think these were merely the most basic courtesies between Harbingers. Now I am nothing, and a wanted fugitive besides...
Yet even now, they still help me. That means what I believed before was wrong. 😌
I feel a little guilty—😞 guilty that I never understood how precious these things were. 😔

Yes. After some thought, I believe you, Arlecchino, and I should count as "friends." 😘
I consider that very important, so I came to tell you. 🥰
Would you rather not be my "friend"? 🥺
Or would you still prefer that we interact only as colleagues? 😞

Will you invite me to your next tea party? 🤗
Mm. I think you will. 😌
Goodbye, dear Sandrone. ☺️

## Ode to Natlan's Flame

We inherit memory and legend!
We grow with the sun and the wind!
We forge destiny and the future!
All these are Natlan's fire—Natlan's blood!

*Hadithi yaendelea*
The story goes on, just like the flame—

*Heshima warithi vizazi*
Glory passes through the generations, burning without end—

*Ushujaa waangaza mbingu na ardhi*
Courage lights heaven and earth—

*Mara tena ashinda Natlan*
Once more, the world welcomes victory—

*Milele tuwangoje, na tutawaimbia*
We wait for you; we sing for you—

*Rudini ndugu, rudini mashujaa*
Return, my friends; return, heroes—

*Mara ya tena mwangani*
Once more, once more—burn—
