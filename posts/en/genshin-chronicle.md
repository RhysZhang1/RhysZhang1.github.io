## Reflections

People hurry through life for no more than a few scraps of silver. Yet those very scraps can ease the sorrows of the world, keep our parents well, and shelter our children as they grow.

Those same scraps of silver can sever childhood dreams, weather the young before their time, and bend a once-straight back.

## Words from the Journey

> I have gradually come to understand that the things which defy description and remain forever in flux are the deepest mysteries in this world.

> Go forth and seek it. Even if the light lasts only an instant, at least this moment is ours.

> To me, constancy is beautiful too. But what in this mortal world can truly remain unchanged?

> When power reaches its limit, what is tested next is the human heart.

> Witnesses come to bear witness; those who remember are born to preserve remembrance.

> If you rush only toward the destination, you may miss the opportunities and emotions waiting along the road.

> Life will always hold things we regret, but we live so that we may leave no regrets behind.

> On the road toward that person, you will encounter still more hardship. Yet so long as you believe the road leads somewhere right, all of it has meaning.
