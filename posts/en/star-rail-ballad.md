# Songs of the Star Rail

## Imitation

I kept learning from them, imitating them, copying them as closely as I could.
I pretended to be them and dreamed that one day I might take their place.
There were times when I made you happy too—weren't there?
My imitation was nothing but a joke.
I tried so hard to become them, hoping I could help you forget.
Instead, I only made you remember them again and again.
Perhaps that is the price of taking a shortcut.

---

Not every eagle that falls with broken wings will rise again,
but unless it risks the fall, it will never touch the sky.

Moving forward does not bring loss; it also creates new encounters.
If you rush only toward the destination, you may miss the chances and emotions along the road.

## Ode to the Stars

**Justice**
The court divides right from wrong; the theater is packed from wall to wall.
Behold—the guilty turn in masquerade, while rain descends in boundless sheets.

**Wisdom**
Depths of knowledge lie concealed beneath an innocent face, serene as ever.
Though once constrained by wolves and tigers, compassion did not change.
Measureless is the Great Law.

**Eternity**
Thunder shatters the court; majestic light fills heaven, one short blade casting a ten-thousand-foot gleam.
For all this life, grant every living soul the right to dream.

**Contracts**
The heavenly law decrees that transgressors shall fall.

**Civil and Martial Virtue**
Peerless in both letters and arms; benevolence wins all hearts within the seas; achievements worthy of an emperor through the ages.

**Freedom**
Freedom rides upon the fragrance of wine, adorning a verdant crown of windblumes.
Let the wind point the way; let its intoxicating voice sing.

**The Curse**
Calamity stands before the world; on and on she seeks a cure.
Rather than weep alone upon the throne, what matter if she casts away her crown?

**Legacy**
Pass down the word; let the righteous lend their aid. Restore the throne and cleanse the court of flatterers.
Unwilling to forget the mother who gave him life, he falls silent, eyes brimming with tears.

**Protection**
The Eagleplume string resounds in defense of the realm; a sword points at the great serpent, though no arm can be saved.
How I miss the days when we gathered together beneath the cherry blossoms.

**The War for Dominion**
Nine pillars rise; he entered the contest himself and swept the eight wilds.
Beneath Guyun, the gods were laid to rest; Guili remembers one emperor and three kings.
Could we ever share fine wine again?

**Heroic Souls**
They cleared the bitter mountain cold and crushed the venomous dragon's rage.
In the bright mirror, a dear friend's face still wears the look of youth.

**Divinity**
A god is radiance without bound; a god is shelter in every direction.
Despite heartbreak, tears, and wounds, they never betrayed the faith of their people.

## Song of the Astral Express

Gaze upon the galaxy's scattered light; the Express departs along its winding trail.
Across the cosmos and over frozen plains, beside the Xianzhou it soars through boundless sky.
Record each story and seal it in vinyl.
The finale flows into a brand-new chapter; beyond the screen, you and I meet,
walking onward shoulder to shoulder.

Dissolve among the butterflies; the Lightning-Lord blazes—heed my word.
One hundred percent weakness broken; who sleeps within the rosewood coffin?
This lovely scene shall bury you; fulfill the prophecy of fate.
Take up the Vidyadhara crown again.

See all with the Dharma eye; hear the sound that tears the body apart—listen to the Samudrartha.
Within the score written among stars, uncover the secret of the warp.
Life sheltered beneath the snow: only Wildfire remains unquenched.
All shall honor the decree of these wings as we trailblaze through foreign worlds.

Beneath a hundred years of ice and snow, history appears anew.
We storm the space station and lose our account; within preserved memories, smoke rises from an ancient lane.
Every last piece of Aetherium is ours.

If a century changes all the world, the storm will still yield to tomorrow's dawn.
The furnace breaks and heliobi scatter; a livestream hunts the paranormal.
Does that Aeon know? Does that pure beauty know?
The crown of mortal and divine—welcome the audience of Destruction.

Cold moonlight glances from the sword; the hour of reckoning comes.
Subduing demons and catching ghosts, a talisman flashes between the fingers.
Follow the vow of Beauty; an orchid fragrance drifts across the strings.
Wear the name of fool, and prove the face of truth.

Find the trail of vanished friends; cherish kin with a humane heart.
Father and daughter, their love unbroken; memories of home make parting hard.
Leave old wounds and prejudice behind—machines can feel as well.
Seek the road ahead, debate the sword, heal the hurt, and listen to a fool's confession.

Where does the stranger's road end? The newly born do not fear the open sky.
Truth or lie, this journey bids the former years farewell.
Spread hope with iron fists; explore the limits of memory.
With dreams at heart, cross the frozen plain; old companions drink no more beneath today's moon.

Run toward the splendor of the years, where memories weave a scroll.
Stars sink, the moon shines; leap beyond the horizon.
Run toward the world's abundance, while gods whisper low.
From infinite possibilities, fashion something lofty and far.
