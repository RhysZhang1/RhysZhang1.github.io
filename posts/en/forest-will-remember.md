# The Forest Will Remember Everything

Grow up strong and well.
Do not yield to the wind,
nor to the rain,
nor to winter snow,
nor to summer's blaze.

Young one, rejoice while you are still a child.
Let your heart be glad;
walk where you wish to walk,
see what you wish to see—
yet remember, too, the days of darkness.

May new dreams never be corroded by the Withering.
May old stories be forgotten along with it.
May the green fields
and hills never fade to yellow.
May the streams run clear forever.
May the flowers forever bloom.

Dear friends will walk together once more beneath the forest's dense canopy. Everything beautiful will one day return,
and every painful memory will recede—as a stream makes itself pure, and a withered tree puts forth new buds.

In the end, the forest will remember everything.
