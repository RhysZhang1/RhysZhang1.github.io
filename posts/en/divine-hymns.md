# Hymns to Divinity

## Hymn to Senadina

No! ...Senadina is different... Senadina alone is different—😡

Senadina... is unlike anyone else. 😌
Because Senadina's name... sounds so beautiful. Speaking it makes me feel safe. It is the holiest melody in the world... I do not want to shorten it. 🤗
Senadina is the true goddess of Luoxing. She chose this star and kindled the flame buried deep below; the barren planet gained a dancing heart. 🥰
She watches over life and stands guard over civilization. She loves us without reserve, revealing her joy with perfect sincerity before all that rests beneath her protection. 😄
She is the loveliest goddess in the empty universe. Time can never make her wither. Remember her existence with me, and never again forget her name. 😍

Senadina is the true goddess of Luoxing. She engraved truth and humble love upon our hearts, so that ignorant life awakened from chaos and the strings of blessing rang throughout the mortal world. 😘
She shelters the past and embraces the lost. She gave everything to love us, guiding the rightful path with her noble sacrifice and granting you and me her greatest promise. 😫
She is the gentlest goddess in the empty universe. May the world repay her with prosperity that never ends. Remember her existence with me, and never again forget her name. 😄

Flowers flourish, leaves grow thick, and living things crowd the earth beneath our feet—Senadina, this is the treasure you wanted to protect, the gift you left for me.
You said our footprints covered all of Luoxing. When I continue down this road and see lush trees and flowers, clouds across the blue, and faces brimming with happiness, I will meet you once more. 😢

Yes. Your shadow lies everywhere here; of course I can see it. But this was never what I longed for... 😭
I want the Senadina who shared the same view with me across the vast sea of stars, who stayed by my side through the long years. I want to return to the days when you were within reach and truly here with me. 😢
Senadina... If I had asked you then—please, do not leave me—would you have stayed? 😭

## The First Cause

Bear witness—to the First Cause!

If you and I had never been alone, why would we have taken roads that carried us ever farther apart? 😥

Let our final negotiation end here. 😡

The work of all creation is complete. 😤
The day beyond doubt has arrived. 😃
Fetus of philosophy. 🤓

Remake heaven, earth, and every form of creation for us! 🌞

If your paradise can save still more people, 😏
then cut off the road before me with your own hands! 😱

On the first day, grant "truth." 🌞
On the second day, grant "the calendar." 🌞
On the third day, grant "language." 🌞
On the fourth day, grant "value." 🌞
On the fifth day, grant "rules." 🌞
On the sixth day, grant "meaning." 🌞
On the seventh day, grant "dignity." 🌞

By this seven-day oath, I command you to obey. 😃

It was not you who fashioned all creation! 😡
Humanity remade *you*! 👊😡

O fallen Aeon, I salute you! 😤

With your divine body, lay the foundation of our paradise! 🌞👉👈😃
💥💥💥

**The First Cause!!!**
